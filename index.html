The History of Cedar Point

Cedar Point has undergone many changes starting as a simple beach and bathouse in 1870 to becoming the amusement park it is today. It served as the "flagship park" of the Cedar Fair amusement park chain prior to the merger with Six Flags in 2024. The park features 18 roller coasters, which ties for first among amusement parks in North America alongside sister parks Six Flags Magic Mountain and Canada's Wonderland. Here's a look back at the park's unique history in a timeline featuring enhancements and changes to America's Roller Coast!

Key Years in History 1870-1969

Cedar Point went through many important changes from 1870-1969

  • 1870: Cedar Point opens as a public bathing beach. First passengers arrive at Cedar Point Resort on the Steamer, "Young Reindeer."
  • 1882: Dock built at Biemiller's Cove area; new dance hall and eight bathhouses also built there.
  • 1888: The enormous Grand Pavilion opens, featuring an auditorium, bowling alleys and dining facilities.
  • 1892: The park's first roller coaster, the Switchback Railway, opens. It stood an amazing 25 feet tall and reached an unbelievable top speed of 10 mph.
  • 1899: Cedar Point opened its first hotel, the Bay Shore Hotel.
  • 1905: One of the largest hotels in the Midwest: historic Hotel Breakers welcomes its first guests.
  • 1906: A new midway is built featuring various rides, games, fortunetellers, merchandise shops, a skating rink, a massive Coliseum with a grand ballroom and other attractions.
  • 1914: On July 19, the roadway (Cedar Point Chausee) was opened to the public.
  • 1968: Cedar Point's live entertainment department is formed. Frontier Town opens.
  • 1969: Town Hall Museum opens in Frontier Town; displays photos and memorabilia from the park's colorful past.

Important Rides Built

Cedar Point opened many record breaking rides from 1870-1969

  1. Switchback Railway opened in 1892
  2. Figure-Eight Roller Toboggan opened in 1902
  3. Cyclone roller coaster opened in 1929
  4. Midway Carrousel opened in 1946
  5. Cadillac Cars, Scrambler and Tiki Twirl opened in 1960
  6. Sky Ride and Star Voyager opened in 1961
  7. Sky Wheel opened in 1962
  8. Cedar Point & Lake Erie Railroad and Mill Race opened in 1963
  9. The oldest operating roller coaster at Cedar Point today, the Blue Streak, and Western Cruise (subsequently named Paddlewheel Excursions) opened in 1964
  10. Earthquake, Space Spiral, Turnpike Cars, and Jungle Larry's African Safari opened in 1965
  11. Pirate Ride opened in 1966
  12. Cedar Downs Racing Derby and the Rotor opened in 1967
  13. Shoot-the-Rapids, the Frontier Lift, Kiddieland Carrousel and Sky Slide opened in 1968
  14. The Cedar Creek Mine Ride, one of the first roller coasters to utilize steel tubular track, opened as well as Antique Cars in 1969
