Key Years in History 2000 - Present

Cedar Point went through many important changes from 2000-Present

  • 2000: Cedar Point stuns the coaster world when it debuts the tallest (310 feet) and fastest (93 mph) "giga-coaster" on the planet, Millennium Force.
  • 2001: The charming Lighthouse Point opens, featuring 50 cottages, 10 cabins and 59 luxury RV campsites.
  • 2002: The park debuts the Wicked Twister roller coaster.
  • 2003: Cedar Point shocks thrill-seekers yet again with the debut of the 420-foot-tall, 120-mph Top Thrill Dragster - making Cedar Point home of the tallest and fastest roller coaster in the world.
  • 2004: Cedar Point invests nearly $10 million in capital improvements for its 2004 season with expansions at its Lighthouse Point luxury camping complex and Soak City waterpark. Cedar Point also makes a big splash with the addition of its new $22 million Castaway Bay indoor waterpark resort.
  • 2005: Cedar Point takes its guests to new heights with the introduction of its 68th ride - maXair.
  • 2007: Named “Best New Ride of 2007"; the Maverick roller coaster was introduced.
  • 2013: Located at the new front entrance, GakeKeeper forever changed the landscape of the park. This mammoth ride opened as the longest wing roller coaster and boasted the longest drop of any wing coaster on Earth!
  • 2016: Cedar Point unleashed Valravn, the tallest, fastest and longest dive roller coaster in the world!
  • 2018: Another "world's first" opens at the park - Steel Vengeance, the tallest, fastest, longest, steepest hybrid roller coaster on Earth. The coaster is so large, another new roller coaster category is defined: the "hyper-hybrid." Steel Vengeance is named “Best New Ride” of 2018 and continues to get awarded year after year.
  • 2023: The park's heydey is reimagined with a lakeside midway transformation known as The Boardwalk.
  • 2024: Racing action returns to Cedar Point with the debut of Top Thrill 2, the world's tallest & fastest triple-launch strata coaster.
